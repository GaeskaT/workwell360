WorkWell 360 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

WorkWell 360 ("the app") is a workplace mental-wellness companion. This policy explains what data the app handles and how your privacy is protected.

1. Data stored on your device

By design, your personal wellbeing data — daily check-ins, journals, self-assessment results, diaries, trackers, and any notes you write — is stored only on your own device using your browser's local storage. This data is not transmitted to us or to your employer.

2. What your employer can and cannot see

Where an organisation provides WorkWell 360, your employer never receives your individual check-ins, journals, assessments, or counselling conversations. Your personal wellbeing data is not shared with your employer.

3. Counselling

If you request a counselling session, the information you choose to share is provided only to the counsellor you select. Clinical notes and client identities are kept confidential.

4. Your control

You can export or permanently erase all of your data at any time from Settings → Your data. Erasing removes it from your device and cannot be undone.

5. This is not emergency or medical care

WorkWell 360 offers self-help support and does not provide medical diagnosis. If you are in crisis, use the in-app "Get urgent help" screen or contact your local emergency services.

6. Changes

If future versions add accounts, payments, or cloud sync, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and why, before those features are enabled.
